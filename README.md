# Brain Hub

A personal command center for students. Fork it to your own GitHub, open it
on your phone or laptop, and run AI tools: company analysis, job prep, and
tools you build yourself.

*Un centro de mando personal para estudiantes. La guía de inicio está en
español.*

# 🚀 → **[START HERE](START-HERE.md)** · **[EMPIEZA AQUÍ](EMPIEZA-AQUI.md)** ←

About 15 minutes, all doable on a phone: make your copy, turn on its website,
set it up, and run your first sourced company analysis.

---

- **Works with or without an Open Brain.** With a brain, tools pull in what
  you already know and can save their results to it.
- **Works with or without paying for AI.** Manual mode copies the prompt into
  the AI app you already use, for free. Automatic mode uses your OpenRouter
  key.
- **Every answer is checked.** The hub counts which claims have a source, and
  the Checker can have your AI open every link to confirm it.
- **Build your own tools.** Paste `WIDGET-GUIDE.md` into any AI and ask it
  for a tool.

**Status:** Phases 1–5 complete; Phase 7 (student pilot) is next. See `docs/`.

## Where things go

| Folder | Whose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `core/` | maintainer | Don't edit it, or "Sync fork" will conflict. |
| `plugins/` | yours | Put your own tools here. |

Your keys and password are stored in your browser only, never in this repo.
